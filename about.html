About

A career built across science, training, compliance and technology.

I began in veterinary science and clinical animal health, working in veterinary nursing when I graduated in Veterinary Biology in 2018. I later completed my veterinary nursing qualification and worked across veterinary technician responsibilities, particularly emergency and critical care, pathology, diagnostic imaging and surgery.

Alongside that clinical path, my role within a maritime RTO expanded across educational resources, active training and assessment, compliance, aXcelerate, website development, marketing, project delivery and operational improvement.

My strength is the ability to understand how regulation, systems, people, learning and communication fit together, then build something practical that works.
