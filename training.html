Training & Assessment

Practical training designed around the learner and the industry.

I actively deliver and assess First Aid and CPR training and developed a practical workshop approach based on realistic scenarios, demonstrations, hands-on practice and learner decision-making.

How I train

Rather than relying on passive presentation-based delivery, I structure sessions so learners practise the skills, discuss realistic situations and connect training to their own work environment.

  • Practical scenarios
  • Hands-on demonstrations
  • Industry-relevant examples
  • Group activities
  • Competency-based assessment
  • Learner coaching
  • Reasonable adjustment
  • Continuous improvement

Delivery informs design.

Because I actively deliver and assess, I can see where instructions are unclear, timing is unrealistic, an assessment does not flow well, or learners need a better explanation.

Training should not simply be attended and passed. It should be experienced, understood and remembered.
