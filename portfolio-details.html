Case study

The problem

First Aid training can become passive and compliance-driven when learners sit through generic content that feels disconnected from their workplace. I wanted the workshop to feel practical, participatory and relevant while still meeting the nationally recognised unit and assessment requirements.

My approach

I structured delivery around realistic emergency scenarios, demonstrations, hands-on practice, group activities, learner questioning and decision-making. Examples and scenarios were adapted to the cohort and industry context without changing the required competency outcomes.

What I created

  • Practical workshop structure and session sequencing
  • Scenario-based learner activities
  • Presentation and demonstration resources
  • Assessment flow and practical observation processes
  • Learner coaching and support approach
  • Continuous-improvement changes based on actual delivery and feedback
