What makes my background useful

I understand training from both sides: I design resources and systems, but I also stand in front of learners and deliver and assess training in practice. That means I can see where an assessment looks compliant on paper but does not flow well in a real workshop.

I also understand the systems around training — enrolment, onboarding, LLN, aXcelerate, WordPress, learner communications, surveys, certification, compliance evidence and continuous improvement.

My veterinary background adds another layer: emergency and critical care, pathology, diagnostic imaging and surgery taught me to work from evidence, communicate clearly, document accurately and make practical decisions under pressure.
